Headset

(wired) for landline phone

Moving text with smooth animation

The headset will be useful if you have to talk over a wired telephone line while viewing something on a computer.. Pressing the receiver to your ear is convenient, of course, but not so much, to be honest.

Professional headsets are expensive, somewhat large, and the earphone covers the ear - it is more comfortable with an in-ear earphone.
The headset circuit is designed based on the schematics of various wired handsets.

Headset operation: 
it is assumed that there is a phone somewhere; when it rings, the headset connects to the telephone network, the network perceives this as lifting the handset, and upon finishing the conversation, the headset disconnects from the network (a toggle switch can be installed)

TO VIEW THE FULL-SCALE IMAGE, CLICK ON THE PICTURE

  Electret microphone,  6mm in diameter

  telephone network connects to pins 1,2 (polarity does not matter)

  earphone connects to pins 3, 4.

  A dialer can also be connected to the circuit, which will control   transistors VT1, VT2, but in this case these transistors and diodes VD1, VD2 must be replaced with higher voltage ones



External appearance of the headset:

The board with the microphone can be placed in heat shrink tubing.

The use of SMD made it possible to manufacture a compact board.

As can be seen in the picture, by using a piece of heat shrink tubing (black segment) part of the earphone  wire  is shaped like an ear and rigidity is added - the earphone does not just  get inserted into the ear, but frames the ear.


  Top and bottom view of the board 

 Finished headset. From the board with the microphone (it is in the black heat shrink on the left)  there are two wires going to the earphone (the original earphone cable), two wires (twisted pair) and a steel wire soldered to the board - its opposite end is inserted into the tube of the earphone housing
